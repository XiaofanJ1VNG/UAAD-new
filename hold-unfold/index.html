Hold/Unfold

3D-Printed Folding Fan

Hold/Unfold is a portable gesture of connection and collectivity made for embodied spaces: the underground dance floor, the heat of a summer gathering, anywhere gesture and proximity shape experience. Each blade an abstract body: folded, a single figure; opened, a chain of interlinked forms in embrace, circling a shared center.

Made by Underground Art And Design, a curatorial platform uplifting experimental practices that reshapes what's emerging in art, technology and culture through relational, collaborative work.

Fan Care & Reassembly Guide

01

This is what your fan should normally look like.

02

If the fan comes apart, don't worry! It's designed to be easily reassembled.

03

Gently spread the blades apart in a direction perpendicular to their length, then overlap and stack them back together.

04

Slide the blades to the left until they can't move any farther. Once they stop, they're locked back into place!
